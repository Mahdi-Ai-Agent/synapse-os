# Code of conduct

Synapse follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and language (English and Persian are both at home here), and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security advisory draft on this repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
