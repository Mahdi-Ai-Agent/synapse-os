# Insights — file format

Replace `memory/insights.md` wholesale (labels in the UI language; titles as written):

```markdown
# Insights — 2026-10-31

## Summary
Three to five sentences: who they are as the vault shows them this month, where their energy actually goes, and the one change that would matter most.

## Strengths
- what they keep doing well, each with its evidence: [[Title]], a streak, a finished project

## Patterns to watch
- a loop or a weak spot with its evidence: tasks that keep slipping ([[Title]] due 2026-10-26), a goal paused for weeks, a category with no movement since …

## Where your time should go
- three items with why: a goal behind its target, a project with no next action, a relationship going quiet

## Finish or let go
- items open too long: finish, reschedule, or drop — one line each

## Questions for you
- three questions only they can answer

## Since last time
- what changed since the previous insights (or "first insight")
```

Rules:
- Evidence from the vault only; every bullet links a node or names a count. Never invent, never diagnose, never moralize; kind and direct.
- At most 60 lines; three bullets per section is enough.
- Persian labels: خلاصه · نقاط قوت · الگوهایی که باید حواست باشد · وقتت کجا باید برود · تمام کن یا رها کن · سؤال‌هایی برای خودت · نسبت به دفعه‌ی قبل.
