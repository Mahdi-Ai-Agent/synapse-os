# Full review — rules

"Untouched N" means `updated` is more than N days before **Now**. Never change a node updated in the last 3 days except to link it. When a rule fits but the call is not obvious, do not act: put one question under **Asked of you** in `memory/focus.md`.

## 1. Archive what is obsolete (status only; the item moves to the map's archive corner and out of the daily index)

| Node | Set | When |
|---|---|---|
| task `todo`/`doing` | `dropped` | due 60+ days ago and untouched 60, and its project is done, archived or missing |
| idea / note | `archived` | untouched 120, nothing active links to it, not in an active project or goal |
| resource `active` (saved for later) | `archived` | untouched 180 |
| habit `paused` | `archived` | untouched 60 |
| project `paused` | `archived` | untouched 90 and no open task |

Never archive people, areas, maps, journal days, events (they are the user's history), the character map's items, or anything an open task, an active project or an active goal links to. At most 25 archivals per review. Done and achieved work retires by itself after 30 days; do not touch it.

## 2. Priorities (`priority` 1–4 on every open task; 1 = highest)

1. Overdue, due within 2 days, or the next step of a goal behind its `target`.
2. Due this week, or the next action of an active project.
3. A later due date, or it serves an active goal.
4. Everything else.

Write `priority` only when it differs from what the task has.

## 3. Goals and projects

- Active goal: set `progress` from its tasks and projects (done ÷ all, as a percentage) when they measure it; otherwise keep it, and ask under **Asked of you** when it has not moved in 30 days.
- Active project with no open task: create its clearest next action as a task (`source: brain`, `priority: 2`, `project: "[[Project]]"`), or ask when there is none to see.
- A goal whose `target` date has passed below 100%: ask (extend, change, or let it go). Never decide it.

## 4. Links

For nodes created or updated since the date of the last `memory/focus.md` (all, on the first review) that link to nothing: link them as `/link` does — their category's sub-index first, at most 5 candidates each, at most 30 new links in all.

## 5. Places

An item with no category, or one whose index line plainly belongs to another category, goes there (`area`, or through its project); at most 15 moves. Two nodes that look like duplicates: ask, never merge.
