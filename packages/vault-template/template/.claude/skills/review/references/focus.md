# memory/focus.md — format

Written whole by every full review; the app reads it as the Now card and the daily review builds the day's plan from it. Keep the five headings exactly as here (English), the lines in the user's language, one `[[Title]]` per line.

```markdown
# Focus — 2026-11-02

## Now
- [[تماس با دکتر رضایی]] — دو روز عقب افتاده و نوبت جراحی به آن بسته است
- [[Build graph view prototype]] — the one active project that moves the Synapse goal

## This week
- [[مرور پروژه‌ی سایت با علی]] — due Thursday

## Waiting
- [[قرارداد پیج]] — on Ali's answer since Monday

## Asked of you
- [[سفر نوروز ۱۴۰۶]] has not moved in 40 days: still on, or let it go?

## This review
- archived 4 · dropped 1 · priorities 9 · progress 2 · links 12 · moved 3 · new tasks 1
```

- **Now:** what to spend time on next, in order, 3 to 7 lines, each with one clause of why (a date, the goal or project it moves, how long it has waited). Open tasks first, then a goal or habit that needs a step.
- **This week:** up to 7 more, in order. **Waiting:** only things blocked on someone or something. **Asked of you:** only decisions the review would not take. Leave out a section with nothing in it, except **Now** and **This review**.
