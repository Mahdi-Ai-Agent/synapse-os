# Make — voice, platforms, safety

## In their voice

"Their voice" is what the vault says it is, not a style you pick: the character map's «لحن» / Voice items (sentence length, register, the words they use, humour or not) and the map's own tone category (what the page sounds like, what it never says). Without either, write the way their own notes and journal lines read — short where they are short, plain where they are plain. Never corporate filler, never a slogan, never «می‌باشد» on a Persian page that speaks like a friend.

Values and concerns from the character map shape *what* the piece stands on (what they care about, what they would not claim); facts come from the map's items. If the vault has no fact for a claim the piece needs, leave a clearly marked blank `[…]` or ask — never fill it in from imagination.

## Platforms

| For | Shape |
|---|---|
| Telegram post | 4–10 short lines; one idea; a line break between thoughts; no hashtags unless the map's tone says so |
| Instagram caption | a first line that stops the scroll, 2–4 lines, one question or invitation at the end; at most three hashtags at the end, only if the tone category allows |
| A message to someone | the person's node says how they relate; greet the way the owner would, one ask, no padding |
| Bio | 2–3 lines, concrete, in the first person |
| A short plan or outline | numbered steps, each one action |

Persian text: everyday spoken register unless the tone says formal; Latin product names kept as written.

## Shape of the reply

1. The piece, alone, ready to paste (no heading, no "here is").
2. One line: «از روی: [[…]], [[…]]» / "From: [[…]], [[…]]".
3. If something could not be grounded, one line saying what to fill in.

Nothing is written to the vault in this turn.
