# Events (moments)

- An event is something that happened: a dinner, a hard conversation, a trip day, good or bad news. `date: YYYY-MM-DD` (resolve "yesterday", «دیروز» from Now), `people` for who was there, `place` when stated, `mood`: `great` · `good` · `okay` · `low` · `bad` — the user's feeling about it, only when they express one.
- Body: what happened and what the user felt or learned, in their words and language; kind and factual, never judging. No advice unless asked.
- Feelings about a relationship belong here (an event linked to the person), while lasting facts about the relationship go on the person node.
